# Arcium 0.2.0-preview.1

October 23, 2026

Our first preview in the Arcium-macOS release home. For Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 26 or later.

## Highlights

- Your Spaces choose the profile. Arcium opens without a profile picker getting in the way.
- Save a split view as one Pin and return to the same pair of pages.
- Make the glass yours. Turn off every glow for pure glass, or choose a flat tint.
- Bring your sidebar and history over from Arc. The import guide covers passwords and optional cookies separately.

## Polish and fixes

- Window and content corners follow native macOS rounding more closely.
- Traffic lights line up at larger UI scales, and command bar colors fade down from the top.
- Typed searches keep their place as the default action, even when an open tab matches.
- Arc imports preserve starter Spaces and give favorites-only profiles a visible home.
